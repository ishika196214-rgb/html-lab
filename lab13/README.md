 Lab 13 - Flexbox

In this lab we learned flexbox.

1. nav-flex.html - Made a nav bar with 4 links. Used display flex, dark background and white text.
2. centred-cards.html - Made a row with 3 cards Starter, Standard, Premium with price 199, 349, 499. Used justify-content center and align-items center to centre them.
3. wrapping-cards.html - Made Stationery Shop with 4 cards Notebook, Pen, Bag, Book. Used flex-wrap so when screen is narrow cards go to next line.

It was interesting to see wrapping.
